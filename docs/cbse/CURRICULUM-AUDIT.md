## Specific cautions

- Class 9 standard includes a shared Earth-system unit (pp13–14). General stage-wide competencies are not themselves the taught chapter list; do not insert an old standalone gravitation chapter into Class 9 on that basis.
- Class 10's detailed formative blocks and broader teacher note conflict; see WORKFLOW.md. Keep uncertain assessment scope visible. The optics scope excludes the sunrise/sunset colour explanation.
- Biology 11: digestion/absorption is explicitly formative-only (p6). Frog systems are the specified animal structural study (p4); other dissections remain enrichment. Solanaceae is named for plant-family theory; practical local substitutes are a separate allowance.
- Biology 12: environmental issues are formative-only (p11), not interchangeable with the core ecology/conservation objectives.
- Senior Physics: the document's opening heading retains an older year, while the course tables explicitly identify 2026–27. Use the official current index and course tables together. Respect excluded textbook content (pp11,22).
- Required hands-on practicals and recorded observations cannot be marked complete by opening a simulation.
