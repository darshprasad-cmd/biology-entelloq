# Exterior asset candidates — initial research and current intake

Initial research below is historical, not current download status. By September 13, the first frog, cockroach, earthworm, Artec fish and heart archives had been inspected. The CC0 black-spotted pond frog and CC BY cockroach are prepared and integrated; the other reviewed assets were unsuitable for functional replacement. All five specimens are now active again. Frog source: [CC0 black-spotted pond frog by ffish.asia/floraZia](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cc0-black-spotted-pond-frog-8cfb74fe45684601863b8a43c8ed9373). Use the author-supported download flow, not extracted viewer payloads. Sketchfab documents its [authenticated download API](https://sketchfab.com/developers/download-api); per-model formats/archives still require verification. Do not incorporate noncommercial/no-derivatives models into a redistributable commercial application without appropriate rights.

Asset intake must record source, author, license, species, units, texture maps, mesh topology, file hashes, modifications, budgets and attribution. Then verify cuts, forceps removal, pin anchors, organ relationships, preview visibility, reload cleanup and both intact/opened appearance. Existing-source 3D files from the user remain preferable for an exact reference reconstruction.
