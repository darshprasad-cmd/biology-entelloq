## Scope and prerequisites

The reference is the user's 32-panel storyboard, beginning with “Landing / Hero” and ending with “Learn & Reflect.” Its images communicate the desired finish and interactions; they are not evidence of existing features or an authoritative anatomy source.

- Keep the current global app layout, routes, dark-green identity, organ anchors, and existing feature contracts. Panel 1 means a **lab-local entry/hero**, not a redesign of the global landing page or other learning sections.
- Await the original `.blend`, `.glb`/`.gltf`, or `.fbx` files, texture maps, species information, and modification/commercial-redistribution license. A PNG cannot supply hidden geometry, separate organs, UVs, material maps, or deformation topology.
- Retain stable part IDs, layers, systems, cuttable/detachable flags, incision metadata, and pickable meshes. Preserve authored organ-relative transforms; adapt a new exterior around the existing anatomical anchors, rather than moving organs to fit an unrelated scan.
- First approve one frog's intact and opened appearance, at the actual desktop/mobile camera views. Only then extend the approved asset/material process to all five specimens.
- A photo-reference mode must say what it shows. Camera compositing is not registered AR, and a simulated X-ray is not a real scan of an animal on camera.
- Do not flatten the storyboard into a bitmap with pretend controls. Each visible control must operate real scene state, with a working exit/reset and honest availability state.

## Existing coverage gaps, not new release claims

- **Tutor coverage:** `TUT_BANK` contains frog/heart questions only (`tutor.js:215` onward); eligibility requires `q.spec === specimen` (`tutor.js:1194`). Fish, worm, and roach do not inherit suitable questions automatically.
- **Viva integration:** `main.js:1401` passes transcript/grade/findings as a second argument, but `shell.showViva(state)` (`shell.js:1198`) does not consume it. Its non-frog branch uses heart questions for every other specimen. This is an existing gap, not a completed assessment system.
- **Physiology coverage:** `PHY_PROFILE`, vascular and neural graphs are frog/heart only (`physio.js:87`, `physio.js:151`, `physio.js:279`). `createPhysiology` falls back to frog for other specimen IDs (`physio.js:531`). Do not claim accurate all-five living physiology; unsupported species need an honest disabled state or reviewed models.
- **Inspection state is coupled to dissection:** imaging swaps materials/visibility and explicitly pauses dissection (`imaging.js:30`, `main.js:989`); layer progression, removed parts, deformation rest geometry, and constraints each retain state. New isolation, cross-section, focus, explode, or comparison modes cannot independently mutate these fields and expect safe restoration.

## Proposed implementation sequence — requires architecture review

1. **Asset intake and single-frog approval.** Inspect supplied files, textures, license, species, mesh separation, units, UVs, topology and budget. Map stable part IDs and freeze existing organ anchors. Produce intact/opened frog appearance checkpoints in the existing lab; obtain appearance approval before multiplying work.
2. **Reversible inspection foundation.** Design one lab mode/state owner for dissection, section, isolate, system focus, reference media, and comparison. Save/restore camera, visibility, material and interaction ownership; keep inspection offsets out of authored home/rest transforms. Introduce non-destructive view reset, explicit anatomical axes, selected-part focus and correctly capped sections. Shared controls should serve panels 6–10, 15, 19–20, 24–25 and 29–30.
3. **Extend the approved finish to all five.** Adapt each accepted asset to the current part contracts, then author specimen-specific internal views, wing rig, labels, sections and exploded offsets. Validate whole-specimen transforms, organ relationships, cut/peel access, tray seating and switch/reset cleanup. Do not replace a functional multi-part specimen with a single attractive outer mesh.
4. **Species-specific learning and circulation.** Add reviewed flow graphs and valve animation tied to a shared timeline; distinguish measured values from teaching approximations. Add structure questions, feedback and reflection for each species, consume real grade/transcript data, and correct unsupported physiology/viva paths. No all-five parity claim until all-five content and controls are verified.
5. **Registered AR as a separate capability.** Review camera permissions, supported browsers/devices, world placement versus tracked-real-specimen registration, anchors, calibration, occlusion, drift recovery and stop cleanup. A world-anchored virtual frog and anatomy registered onto a physical frog are different deliverables. Neither is achieved merely by drawing the webcam behind the scene. Retain desktop/touch 3D fallback.

## Scientific and acceptance gates

Correct the storyboard's comparison: the bony-fish teaching heart has **two chambers**, the frog heart **three**, and the mammalian heart **four**. These counts and the differing circulation routes are supported by [OpenStax Biology, 40.1: Overview of the Circulatory System](https://openstax.org/books/biology/pages/40-1-overview-of-the-circulatory-system). The isolated frog heart must not reuse a mammalian-looking heart just to match panel 8's artwork.

Keep the visible “illustrative teaching anatomy” disclosure. Species-specific anatomy, reference-media provenance, tissue behaviour, imaging assumptions and physiology limits need review; polished rendering is not scientific validation.

Before claiming completion: verify real controls and state restoration; all-five cut/peel/pick/organ-position contracts; named-plane correctness and section caps; reversible isolate/explode/compare; species-appropriate assessment; keyboard/touch targets and reduced motion; desktop/tablet/mobile layout and device performance. Real camera, AR registration and headset claims require physical-device evidence, not synthetic screenshots or hand-state test data. Release only implemented and verified features; retain this backlog's unbuilt items as proposed.
