# Fish and earthworm external models

The inspected working views showed a fish with broad regular scale arcs across its head, a raised button-like eye and an oval gill cover; the worm's faint annuli and uniform profile read as a polished tube.

The fish now owns a bounded, reusable scale pigment atlas aligned with its body axis. Fine staggered flank scales and a subtle lateral line fade before the smooth cranial region. Both eyes sit against the actual body geometry, with flatter irises, radial pigment, a restrained dark outer edge and a larger pupil. The gill cover keeps its fitted, pickable surface but has a slightly asymmetric contour and a thinner posterior seam. The skin mesh, fins, internal anchors and all original part contracts remain intact.

The worm now has stronger shallow annuli, warmer dorsal pigment and gradual variation in body fullness. The clitellum remains smooth; its shoulders and both terminal pieces use the same profile and pigment field as the body. Opaque material avoids tray colour showing through the tapered ends. No centerline or internal organ position moved. The body keeps its original 8,745-vertex buffer and tissue-response support.

References for the external landmarks: [Florida Museum fish anatomy](https://www.floridamuseum.ufl.edu/discover-fish/fish/anatomy/) and [University of Florida earthworm account](https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/IN946). These changes remain procedural teaching illustrations. They do not resolve the existing mixed-species fish or earthworm anatomy, establish photographic equivalence, or imply measured scale spacing, segment counts, tissue dimensions or preservation colours.

Validation includes original internal-geometry and part-contract hashes, cuttable-vertex limits, fin seating, gill-cover contact, worm seam closure, taper, shared pigments and soft-body recovery. Focused checks cover head masking of the scale atlas, its retention through surface finishing, seated eye profiles and annular pigment contrast. Root coordinates final browser screenshots and interactions after the combined build.
