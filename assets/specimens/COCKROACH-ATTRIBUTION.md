Original SHA-256: `027d5da04d25c77f7e4ef7fb38970f071934e8ce4dda5b44230134768631190a`. The original download is unchanged.

Modified for Biology Entelloq: baked the exterior rig to its static rest pose; separated the five functional lab surfaces; fitted vertices into the existing body/organ reference frame; reduced walking-leg geometry to meet interactive budgets. The original base-colour and normal PNG bytes and UVs are preserved. PBR material factors darken the chitin, reduce normal intensity and make it nonmetallic; the roughness channel is lifted while retaining its variation. One shared material and three shared textures avoid repeated image decoding.

The derivative is an educational exterior, not a species-validated internal reconstruction. The existing organs remain a generalized teaching approximation. The three preview views do not validate camera tracking, cutting or browser performance. Review the actual lab before release.
